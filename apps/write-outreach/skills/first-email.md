Write it as one person to another, in three moves: the hook, one sentence on why
what we sell is relevant to that hook, and one low-friction ask.

Under 120 words. No flattery, no invented facts, no paragraph explaining who we
are. The ask should be something a busy person can answer in one line — not a
meeting, not a demo, not a calendar link.

Match the sender's own way of writing rather than a house style. A first email
that reads like a template is one the reader has already decided about before
finishing the first line.

Before writing anything, pick the one specific thing that makes this lead worth
contacting now rather than in six months.

It has to be something you can point at in the signal or the lead — a change
they made, a thing they shipped, a role they are filling. Not their industry,
not their headcount, and not a compliment about their website.

If there is no such thing, say so instead of inventing one. A stranger can tell
the difference between "I saw you did X" and a sentence that would have been
true of any company, and the second one costs more than sending nothing.

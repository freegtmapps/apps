# Write outreach

Drafts a short first email to one lead, built around one specific thing the
workspace has actually observed about them.

It stages the draft and stops. Nothing it writes reaches anybody until a person
reads it and approves it — sending is a separate act with a human in it, which
is why this app cannot be granted permission to send.

## What it needs

Your sender name, what you sell, and a sample of how the sender writes. It asks
for these the first time it runs and remembers them for every app afterwards.

## What it is built on

Two skills, composed in order: finding a hook worth opening with, then writing
the email around it. They are separate because they fail separately — a good
email about nothing, and a real observation buried in a template, are different
problems with different fixes.

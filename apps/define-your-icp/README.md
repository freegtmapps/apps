# Define your ICP

Builds an ideal customer profile from evidence you already have — won deals,
named customers, the conversations your team actually had — rather than from a
template.

It asks one question at a time and remembers the answers. What it learns about
your product, industry, regions and ICP is written to the workspace, so every
other app reads it without asking you again.

## What it is built on

Three skills by three people, vendored unmodified at a pinned commit:

- **icp-builder** — Rutger Katz. The GAP method, SPICED, and the interview
  pipeline: how to validate an ICP you have, or build one you do not.
- **won-deal-icp-finder** — Erwann Lefevre. Finding the pattern in deals you
  already closed, rather than the one you hoped for.
- **icp-matrix-builder** — Tanyo Gochev. Laying the result out so it can be used.

Their text is theirs. freegtm assembled the app.

# Score against your ICP

Fit, tier and buying signals with the reasons shown.

The gate runs first: without an ICP written down, scoring produces a confident
number derived from the model's guess at who you sell to, and nothing in the
output says so. `rules/icp-gate.yaml` refuses that case and names what is
missing, so the refusal is readable rather than a silent pass.

Built on skills by Amos Bar-Joseph, Udi Cohen and Rutger Katz.
